Who We Are

Partnering to build a world where every neurodivergent person is valued, included and empowered.


TIF4ND brings together families, professionals and communities to create lasting change for neurodivergent individuals. We believe that autism, ADHD, dyslexia and other forms of neurodiversity are natural variations of the human mind, and that every person deserves the opportunity to learn, work and live with dignity.

Our Foundation

Every Mind Matters. Every Life Has Potential.

The Indian Foundation for Neurodiversity (TIF4ND) is dedicated to creating an inclusive society where neurodivergent individuals are respected, supported, and empowered to achieve their full potential.

Through inclusive education, vocational training, family support, and community participation, we aim to create opportunities for independence, dignity, and lifelong belonging.

Our Mission

❝ To empower neurodivergent individuals through inclusive education, vocational opportunities, family support, and community-based care, helping them lead meaningful and independent lives ❞.

Our Vision

❝ A world where every neurodivergent individual has the opportunity to live with dignity, independence, acceptance, and lifelong security within an inclusive and caring community ❞.

Why We Exist

A Community That Cares Beyond Today

One of the biggest concerns for parents of neurodivergent individuals is their child's future when they are no longer around.

TIF4ND envisions a parent-supported inclusive community where families come together to build lasting relationships, shared responsibility, meaningful opportunities, and lifelong support.
