Driving Sustainable Community Development

Transforming Lives & Empowering Communities

The Indian Foundation for Neurodiversity is a non-profit charitable trust dedicated to empowering neurodivergent individuals. The ultimate goal of the organisation is to build an inclusive community that provides lifelong support, security, and integration for the neurodivergent population through vocational training and employment.
